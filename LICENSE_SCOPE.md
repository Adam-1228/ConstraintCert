# ConstraintCert license scope

Copyright 2026 CHI YONG SEONG.

The Apache License 2.0 in `LICENSE` applies to ConstraintCert-authored software,
tests, experiment and analysis drivers, and original reproduction documentation
identified as authored components in an artifact manifest. The author authorized
the recommended project-license/public-artifact direction on 27 September 2026.
This is a license selection; it does not certify a public package or make an
unpublished artifact available.

This grant does not relicense third-party material or publisher-controlled paper
material. In particular:

- The paper's LaTeX source, bibliography, rendered PDF and publication-rights
  boilerplate are outside this software license grant.
- Official PVLDB/ACM template files retain their own terms and are not included
  in the selected supplemental candidate. Their pinned identities remain
  reproduction dependencies.
- The unchanged upstream Milvus configuration retains its upstream Apache-2.0
  terms and exact accompanying license; its provenance is listed in `NOTICE`.
- OSV/PyPI advisory material, CELLAR metadata, bibliographic source exports,
  dependencies and service binaries retain their original terms. A project
  software license is not redistribution clearance for these materials.
- Synthetic result data, corpus-derived records, raw execution evidence and
  operational configuration require file/component-specific attribution and
  release treatment in the final manifest. They are not covered by a blanket
  clearance from this file.

All public distributions must include the applicable licenses and notices.
The public artifact selection and privacy review remain incomplete. No public
archive, artifact URL or final paper submission is established by this file.
